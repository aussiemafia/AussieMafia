Keep it simple

Server rules

Rust is supposed to be dangerous. The rules are there to protect fair play, not remove the game.

  • 01. PvP and raiding are part of Rust.
  • 02. No cheating, scripting or exploiting.
  • 03. No racism or extreme toxicity.
  • 04. Respect the wider community.
  • 05. Admins and moderators do not play on the map.
  • 06. Play hard. Play fair.
