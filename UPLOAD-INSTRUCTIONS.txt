3. Upload the individual files, NOT the ZIP itself and not an extra enclosing folder.
4. Commit the changes, then allow GitHub Pages to publish them.
5. Open your website and refresh with Ctrl+F5 if the old design appears.

index.html must sit beside style.css, app.js, the video, logo and image files.
